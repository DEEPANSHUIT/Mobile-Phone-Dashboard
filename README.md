#  Mobile Phone Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

This project showcases a **Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using **Power BI** for **all ** the mobile devices. The dashboard is an interactive business intelligence tool designed to visualize sales performance, customer behavior, and product demand across various dimensions such as brand, model, city, month, and payment method.

---

## 🔍 Objective

The primary objective of this dashboard is to provide data-driven insights into the performance of Samsung's mobile division. It empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions by understanding:

- Sales trends over time
- Best-selling products
- Customer preferences
- Regional performance
- Payment method usage
- Customer ratings distribution

---

## 📌 Dashboard Components (Detailed Breakdown)

### 🧭 Filter Panel (Top-Level Slicers)

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Brand** | Allows filtering sales data by mobile brand (e.g., Samsung, Apple, OnePlus, Vivo, Xiaomi). |
| **Mobile Model** | Enables deep dive into specific phone models across brands. |
| **Payment Method** | Filters visualizations based on transaction payment types: UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Cash. |
| **City** | Filters the entire dashboard by customer city to assess regional sales patterns. |

---

### 📈 KPI Cards (Top Summary Indicators)

| KPI | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Sales** | Shows total revenue generated (₹710M) from all mobile sales. |
| **Quantity** | Displays total units sold (18K) across all models and cities. |
| **Transactions** | Indicates total number of completed transactions (4K). |
| **Avg Product Price** | Calculates the average selling price of all products (₹40K). |

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of business performance.

---

### 🗺️ Geographical Sales Distribution

- **Total Sales by City (Map View):**
  - A map of India showing bubble plots for cities where transactions occurred.
  - The size and color of each bubble represent the total sales volume.
  - Helps identify high-performing regions for targeted marketing and logistics planning.

---

### 📊 Total Quantity Sold by Month (Line Graph)

- A line graph depicting the **monthly sales volume (units sold)**.
- This trend line is useful for identifying **seasonal trends**, demand fluctuations, and forecasting future sales.
- Includes data points for each month with numerical value labels.

---

### ⭐ Customer Ratings (Bar Chart)

- Vertical bar chart showing the **distribution of customer ratings** from 1 to 5.
- Breakdown:
  - 5 stars: 1384 ratings
  - 4 stars: 789 ratings
  - 3 stars: 599 ratings
  - 2 stars: 502 ratings
  - 1 star: 270 ratings
- Useful for quality assurance, product improvement, and understanding user satisfaction.

---

### 💳 Total Sales by Payment Method (Pie Chart)

- Shows the **percentage breakdown of sales by payment method**:
  - UPI: 23.78%
  - Debit Card: 26.31%
  - Credit Card: 25.52%
  - Cash: 24.39%
- Insights from this help optimize payment options and understand customer preferences.

---

### 📦 Top 3 Models Sold (Horizontal Bar Chart)

- Highlights the three best-selling phone models:
  - **iPhone SE** – 55M
  - **OnePlus 11** – 53M
  - **Galaxy S21** – 51M
- Useful for inventory forecasting and promotional planning.

### 📅 Total Sales by Day Name (Line Graph)

- Visualizes sales distribution across days of the week.

## 💼 Business Use Cases

- 📌 **Sales Forecasting** based on historical monthly data.
- 📌 **Inventory Optimization** by identifying top-performing models and cities.
- 📌 **Marketing Strategy Planning** using day-wise and region-wise trends.
- 📌 **Customer Experience Enhancement** through analysis of ratings.
- 📌 **Payment Channel Optimization** based on preferred modes.
